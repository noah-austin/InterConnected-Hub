## 1. Leadership dashboard — Insights (why the church pays)

| Panel (exact label) | What it measures | What feeds it | Aggregate vs. individual | Why a pastor cares |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Your Year in Review — ready for vision night** (347 needs met · 23 groups started by members · 1,204 new connections · 78% of members active monthly · "Download slides") | Annual totals of community activity | Resolved posts, groups created, first-time connections (comment/message between two members), monthly logins | Aggregate | A vision-night report the church has never had: "here's what the body did for each other this year," with numbers. |
| **New member integration — last 90 days** (Joined → Completed their profile → Joined a group → Posted or commented → Made a real connection) | Where newcomers stall on the way to belonging | Signup date, profile completion, group joins, first post/comment, first connection | Aggregate counts per stage | Assimilation is every pastor's problem; this shows the exact step where people fall off, so the team can intervene. |
| **Group gap analysis — what members want but can't find** ("Moms of preschoolers — searched by 5 members, no group exists" · "Nudge a launch") | Demand for groups that don't exist yet | Search terms and group-search phrases, counted by topic; membership of existing groups | Aggregate (counts by topic; no names) | Ministry planning from real demand instead of guesswork — and a resolved example (Pickleball) shows the loop closing. |
| **Life stage composition** + **Age distribution** | Who the congregation is | Age from onboarding; life stage derived from age + profile (family, student, etc.) | Aggregate percentages | Programming and staffing decisions (young families vs. seniors) made on data, not on who's loudest. |
| **Campus & growth** + **Growth geography — new members, last 90 days** ("Kyle +14 — fastest-growing area, 2.1× last year's pace") | Where members are and where growth is happening | Home campus selection; signup ZIP codes over time | Aggregate | Group placement today, campus planting tomorrow. This line lands hard in Hays County. |
| **Where Grace Community lives** (member map) | Geographic spread of the congregation | Signup ZIP codes, plotted by area (South Austin, Manchaca, Buda, Kyle, Dripping Springs, SW Austin) | Aggregate by area — no addresses, no pins on homes | Most churches have never seen this. Care routes, group locations, where to plant. |
| **Gifts & skills census** ("Teachers & tutors 14 · Healthcare workers 9 · Contractors & trades 8 · Finance 6 · Legal 3 · Creative 7 · Open to mentoring 11") | The occupations and skills sitting in the pews | Occupation, skills, and offerings from member profiles, bucketed | Aggregate counts | "Your church contains 14 teachers and 9 healthcare workers." Instant clarity on what the body can do. |
| **Community health — needs posted vs. resolved** (Apr–Jul bars) | Whether needs are actually getting met, month over month | Request-category posts and "marked as helped" resolutions | Aggregate | Proof the platform is working — and the renewal conversation writes itself. |
| **Pastoral care signals** ("Frank Delgado — last active 34 days ago · usually posts weekly" · "Check in") | Members who have gone quiet relative to their own pattern | Activity **metadata only**: last-active date, posting rhythm, RSVPs, profile completion. **Never message content.** | **Individual-level** — the only panel that names people. Framed on screen as "shepherding, not surveillance." | The one thing a shepherd wants to know and can't: who quietly drifted. |
| **Privacy strip** (closing) | — | — | — | Says in writing: aggregate only, never private messages, never confidential-circle membership, never individual activity beyond what members share publicly. Read it aloud in meetings. |

**Be precise on Care signals.** It is individual-level. That's honest and defensible (it uses the same "last seen" data any app has), but don't describe the dashboard as "100% aggregate." Say: "Every insight is aggregate except one — quiet-member care signals — and that one uses activity dates only, never content."

## 2. Leadership dashboard — admin tools

| Tool (sidebar label) | What it shows / does in the demo | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Stat cards (Active members 348/487 · Posts this week 42 · Needs resolved 31 · New connections 57), 8-week weekly-actives chart, recent-activity feed (a need marked helped, a new member, a file added to a group, an event's RSVP count) | Activity metadata; aggregate plus named activity items that are already public inside the church |
| **Moderation** (badge count) | Anonymous report queue with reason, report count, and member history; one-tap **Dismiss / Send private warning / Remove post / Escalate**; celebratory empty state | Reported post content and reporter count (reporter identity hidden from admins) |
| **Groups** | **Church storage** meter (1.4 of 25 GB, file count) · **New this week** review with **Make official ministry / Looks good / Remove** (no approval queue — members create groups freely) · **All groups** roster with leader, member count, resource count, upcoming events, **Manage / Remove**. Confidential circles show only their name and leader: "Members, posts, files, and events are never visible to admins"; Manage is disabled for them. | Group metadata; files counted for storage, never listed for confidential circles |
| **Members** | Roster (name, occupation, top skills, Active pill) · **+ Invite members** | Profile data members chose to share |
| **Announcements & calendar** | Announcement composer that publishes straight into the member app · **Add to the church calendar** form (name, date, time, place) that appears on every member's phone · list of upcoming church-wide events · past announcements | Church-authored content |
| Cross-demo state | Everything above is live in both directions: badge a group official and it appears on the member Church page; add an event and it lands on the phone calendar; approve Pickleball and members can join it. Show this — it's the best 20 seconds of the admin demo. | — |

## 3. Member app — what a member sees and what we collect

**Collected at onboarding (all shown on screen):** name, email, password · occupation · city & ZIP · age · home campus (South Austin / Buda / Online) · top skills · "what you can offer." Profile later adds: about, spiritual gifts. Privacy toggles: directory visibility, who can message you. **Not collected anywhere:** attendance/check-ins, giving, kids' data, message content for insights.

| Tab / surface | What it does in the demo | Data it creates |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed** | Toggle chips (Main Feed + your groups) blending into one scroll · composer with four categories (Request / Offering / Question / Announcement) · threaded comments and replies · report via ⋯ (anonymous) · resolved posts show "Marked as helped." No algorithm, no "I can help" buttons, no skill-match nudges — by design. | Posts, comments, reports, resolutions |
| **Groups** | My Groups / Available · **Start a new group** (name, description, icon, color, Open / Request to join / **Confidential circle**) — creator becomes leader instantly · tap a joined group for its **headquarters: Feed · Resources · Events** · tap an unjoined group for a preview (description, leader, meeting rhythm; never a member list) · Leader Tools: approve/decline requests, add an event, add a resource, pin/remove files. Resources = documents, spreadsheets, slides, links, with uploader and date. | Group membership, files (names/sizes only in the demo), group events |
| **Search** | Live search of the congregation by name, skill, or occupation → member profile (about, skills, offering, spiritual gifts) with a **Message** button | Search terms (aggregated for gap analysis) |
| **Church** | Church banner with **service times and campuses** · **Give** button · announcements (latest 2, "See all") · **month calendar** with dots colored by group, tap a day, upcoming list, one-tap **RSVP** · **Ministries at Grace** tile grid (the church's official groups — Join / Request / Joined) · **Leadership** directory with Message buttons · **Church resources** (bulletin, sermon notes, start guide) · **Visit & contact** (address, hours, phone, email, Directions, Message the office) | RSVPs, group joins |
| **Profile** | Cover, about, skills, offerings, spiritual gifts, working privacy toggles | Profile fields |
| **Messages** | Floating icon with unread badge · conversation list · sendable threads. Framed as private and encrypted, never visible to admins or staff. | Message content (never surfaced to leadership) |
| Extras | Guided 9-step tour (Rachel's roofer → groups → group HQ → search → church → messages → admin) · sample push notification after onboarding | — |

**The calendar rule (pastors ask):** a member's calendar shows church-wide events plus the groups they belong to. A confidential circle's events, files, and posts are visible only inside it — the demo has planted test cases proving it.

## 4. Do-not-overclaim list — on the landing page but not on the screen

Things the site says that the demo cannot show. Noah can still say them, but as **promises or roadmap**, not "let me show you."

| Claim (where) | Reality in the demo | How to say it |
|---|---|---|
| "Launch Sunday kit: pulpit script, slides, QR codes" (Features, How it launches, FAQ) | A deliverable, not a screen. Nothing to click. | "We provide this with every pilot" — don't imply it's in the software. |
| "Weekly digests instead of alarms" / "We'll email you a weekly digest" (Features, Moderation empty state) | No digest exists or is shown. | Roadmap. |
| "Private encrypted messaging" / "inaccessible to Interconnected staff" (Features, Trust, FAQ) | Messages work; encryption is an architecture commitment, not demonstrable in a prototype. | "Designed to be end-to-end private" — a commitment we'll build to, not a shipped guarantee. |
| "Your branding, your own fully siloed environment" (Features) | Demo shows Grace Community's name and a letter logo; no branding controls exist. | "Each church gets its own branded, siloed instance" — fine as a description of the product model. |
| "Member roster with activity status" (Features) | Every member shows "Active." Invite button is a toast. | Say "roster and invite tools." |
| Privacy toggles "for directory visibility and who can message you" (Features, Trust) | Toggles flip but don't change anything else in the demo (search still lists everyone). | Demonstrate the toggle; don't demonstrate its effect. |
| "Community standards agreed to at signup" (FAQ) | Onboarding has no standards step. | Roadmap — and worth adding; cheap. |
| "Churches can realistically see half the congregation join in the first weeks" (FAQ) | No pilot data exists yet. | Frame as our expectation from the pastor-driven launch model, not a result. Sonny: don't put this in outreach as a statistic. |
| "Escalated to Interconnected support — response within 24h" (Moderation toast) | A support SLA we have not staffed. | Avoid quoting the 24h. |
| "Nudge a launch," "Check in," "Download slides," "Invite members," "Manage," "Give," "Directions," "Open / Share" a file | All toasts — they confirm the action but nothing downstream happens. | Say "in the real product this sends…" |
| "Marked as helped" | Shown on a seeded post; there is no button for a member to mark their own post as helped. | Describe the feature; don't try to trigger it live. |
| "Request to join" a closed group | Sends a toast; the approval side is only demonstrated for a group you create yourself (Tom Alvarez's simulated request). | Create a group live to show the leader side — that part works. |
| Care-signal line "RSVP'd but didn't attend last two events" | Implies attendance tracking, which we don't collect anywhere (no check-in). | Don't claim attendance data. I'll reword this line in the next file. |
| Year in Review "23 groups started by members" vs. roster of 8 | Sample-data mismatch. | If asked, "sample numbers." I'll reconcile in the next file. |
| Hero: "<5 min from invite to first connection" | Marketing claim, not measured. | Fine as aspiration; not a stat. |

**What the demo does fully show, and Noah can lean on:** the feed and threaded conversation · member-created groups with leader tools · group headquarters with files and events · confidential-circle privacy (visible on both sides) · search → profile → message · the full church page including calendar and RSVP · every Insights panel · moderation actions · group oversight · announcements and calendar publishing from admin to phone.
