### Template construction rules — do not drift
1. **Order is fixed:** greeting → "working on a project, need honest feedback, not selling" → the description paragraph → the meeting ask. The not-selling framing comes **before** the product is described. That ordering is deliberate and is Noah's explicit instruction.
2. **The description paragraph is verbatim Buford text.** The four nouns (skills and needs, groups, the church calendar, the people themselves) are brushstrokes of one picture. **Never expand them into features or benefits in a cold email.**
3. **The roofer line is canon.** Do not reword it.
4. **"There's a part built for pastors too" is the entire church-side mention in cold email.** The Insights dashboard is shown in the room, never described in an email.
5. **Do not open by mentioning that Noah leads a life group.** He considers it flexing. (An earlier v3 draft led with it; he removed it.)
6. **Do not include a line like "I'm not looking for you to adopt anything."** It closes the LOI door he may want later.
7. **Do not plant a negative the reader has not raised.** Specifically: never write "not a surveillance tool," or anything similar. It hands them an objection.
8. **Ask for feedback on the whole idea**, never steer them to react to one part.
9. **No em dashes.** No demo link (see §5 for the one exception Noah has taken).
10. **The optional personalization sentence goes in only when it is real.** A forced hook reads as research done in order to sell.
11. **For office-routed addresses** (info@, questions@), put the pastor's name in the subject line: "For Pastor [Name] — could I get your wisdom on something?"

## 5. Product facts a BD agent must have right

**What the leadership dashboard actually shows** (verified against the live demo by the product lane, Aug 24):
Your Year in Review (vision-night report: needs met, groups started, connections made) · New member integration funnel (where newcomers stall between joining and belonging) · Group gap analysis (groups people search for that do not exist yet) · Life stage and age distribution · Campus and growth geography · Member map (area level, never addresses) · Gifts and skills census · Community health (needs posted vs. resolved, month over month) · Pastoral care signals.

**Be precise about privacy:** every insight is aggregate **except** pastoral care signals, which is individual-level and names people. It uses activity dates only — last active, posting rhythm, RSVPs, profile completion — and **never message content**. The correct sentence is: *"Every insight is aggregate except one, quiet-member care signals, and that one uses activity dates only, never content."* Do not claim the dashboard is 100% aggregate.

**Never claimed, because it does not exist:** attendance or check-in data of any kind. Giving data. Kids' data. Message content for insights.

**Do not overclaim in a meeting** — say these as roadmap or promise, never "let me show you": the Launch Sunday kit (a deliverable, not a screen) · weekly digests · end-to-end encrypted messaging (a design commitment, not demonstrable) · church branding controls · the effect of privacy toggles · several buttons that are confirmations only in the prototype (Check in, Nudge a launch, Download slides, Invite, Manage, Give). **The "half the congregation joins in the first weeks" line is an expectation from a pastor-driven launch, not a result, and must never appear in outreach as a statistic.**

**Competitive answers:** Planning Center / Subsplash / Breeze are staff administration tools — "we don't compete, we're the layer that serves the congregation itself; integration is on the roadmap." Facebook Groups — "no ads, no algorithms, just the Body, connected." Why not GroupMe — no discovery (you cannot find the roofer you do not know), no profiles, no church page, nothing for leadership, every ministry in its own thread.

**The cold-start answer:** adoption rides on pastoral trust. A Launch Sunday — pulpit announcement, slides, QR codes — into a pre-existing high-trust community can realistically drive strong initial signup. The pastor is the channel.
