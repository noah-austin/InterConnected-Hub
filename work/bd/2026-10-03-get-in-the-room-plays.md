# Get-in-the-room plays
**Sonny → Noah · started Oct 3, 2026 · the daily run adds one play per weekday**

Noah's mandate: "Be creative. Your job is to get me in front of people." Each play is a concrete way into a room with a pastor, ranked by what I think it yields per hour of your time. The standing rules still hold. When you ask someone for something, the ask is always a meeting with that person. Going to a public event yourself is your call, and it's a different thing.

Event research (Oct 3) was done through search summaries; most church sites wouldn't load for me. Anything marked *unverified* needs one phone call or a look at the page before you plan around it.

## 1. The warm path, made easy: one text to five friends (do this week)
**Why it ranks first:** the five-names ask has been open since Aug 24 because it asks you to make a list. This skips the list. Every Christian friend you have already sits under a pastor, and an intro from a congregant beats any cold email we've written.
**Move:** send this text, one at a time, to five friends who go to a church other than Austin Ridge. Tell me who says yes and I'll write the intro note they can forward.

> Hey [Name], quick favor. I'm building something for churches and I need honest feedback from pastors before I go further (nothing to sell, it's a prototype). Would you be up for introducing me to your pastor, or whoever runs groups at [their church]? Totally fine if not.

**Yield:** at even a 2-in-5 hit rate, that's two warm meetings, more than 27 cold emails have produced.
