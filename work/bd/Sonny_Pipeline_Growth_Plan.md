## 1. The messaging shift (effective now, every new email)
Harlie's reply is one data point, but it matches the risk in the design: a feature paragraph plus "not selling" reads as a pitch with a disclaimer. Home base ratified the church-side line yesterday; this amends *how* it's said, not whether. Three changes to every new first-touch:

1. **Lead with the ask, not the product.** The first real paragraph says "I'm working on a project and need pastors to tell me what's wrong" before the product is named. (The Austin Ridge life-group opener from v3 was dropped by Noah on Aug 26.)
2. **The product gets one picture, not a feature list.** Buford's verbatim paragraph names four nouns (skills and needs, groups, the church calendar, the people) and never expands them. No dashboard tour. "There's a part built for pastors too" is the whole church-side line. The Insights get shown in the room.
3. **Make the ask smaller than it sounds, out loud.** "Nothing to buy, it's a prototype on my phone. I need someone who shepherds a congregation to tell me what's right, what's wrong, and what a church would never use."
