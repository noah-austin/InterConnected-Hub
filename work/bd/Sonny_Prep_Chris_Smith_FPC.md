## Likely objections → responses
- **"Nobody will fill out a profile."** Adoption rides on you, not on the app. A Launch Sunday — pulpit mention, slide, QR code — into a congregation that trusts its pastor can realistically get half the room in. The pastor is the channel.
- **"We already have Church Center."** Not competing. Church Center is where your staff runs the church; this is where the congregation finds each other. Roadmap includes integrating with Planning Center.
- **"Why not a GroupMe / Facebook group?"** No discovery (you can't find the roofer you don't know), no profiles, no church page, nothing for leadership, and every ministry ends up in its own thread. No ads, no algorithm.
- **"Privacy — people won't want their needs visible."** Invitation-only, one church, verified members. Every post is the member's choice; leadership sees aggregate only.
- **"It's not built yet."** True — say it first. "That's why I'm here. I'd rather build it wrong on paper than wrong in code."
