Privacy & trust

Built to be trusted with a church family.

A church platform is only useful if members trust it completely. So the rules are simple, and they're the same for us as for your admins.

Encrypted messages

Personal conversations are private — inaccessible to church admins and to Interconnected staff alike.

One church, one silo

Every church runs in its own isolated environment. Members only ever interact within their own congregation.

Members hold the controls

Each member decides their directory visibility and who may message them.

Confidential circles

Recovery and support groups never expose member lists, posts, or files — not even to church admins. Join requests go only to their leaders.

Aggregate-only insights

Leadership sees the health of the body, never the private conversations within it.

No ads, no algorithms

Nothing farms attention and nothing is sold. The church is the customer — members are never the product.

Common questions

Answers for pastors and boards.

What does it cost, and who pays?

The church subscribes annually — priced by congregation size, in the same range as the giving, streaming, and management tools churches already budget for. Members always join free. An ad-free experience is standard; discounted plans are available for budget-constrained churches.

How much work is this for our staff?

Very little, by design. Moderation reports batch into a weekly digest and resolve in about a minute each. Groups are created and led by members, not the church office. A typical admin spends minutes per week, not hours.

Can leadership read members' messages?

No — and neither can we. Personal messages are encrypted and private, period. Leadership sees aggregate community health, never private content. We believe a church platform is only useful if members trust it completely.

What keeps the feed healthy?

Community standards agreed to at signup, a discreet anonymous report button on every post, and a grace-first moderation flow: private warnings before restrictions, with platform-level support behind your admins for anything serious.

How does a launch actually work?

We provide a Launch Sunday kit — pulpit announcement script, slides, and QR codes. Members scan, create a two-minute profile, and land in the feed. Pastoral introduction is the engine: churches can realistically see half the congregation join in the first weeks.

Where is this today?

Interconnected is in the prototype stage — everything on this page is a live, working demonstration. We're gathering feedback from pastors now and selecting a small group of founding pilot churches ahead of development. Early conversations shape the product.
