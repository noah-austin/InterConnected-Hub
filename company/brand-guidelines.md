# Interconnected — Brand Guidelines v1 (text mirror of Interconnected_Brand_Guidelines_v1.docx)
**Owner:** June (Marketing Director) · **Date:** Aug 24, 2026 · **Status:** canon, all lanes. Changes come from Noah through home base.

*Your church, connected every day of the week.*

## What we are
Interconnected sells a private, invitation-only community platform to individual churches: one siloed network per congregation, where members discover each other's skills, gifts, and needs during the week. Churches subscribe annually; members always join free.

**The spine of every message (two-sided):** members join for community; the church pays for community plus insight (gifts census, member map, integration funnel, care signals, Year in Review).

**Stage, said honestly:** interactive prototype, pastor-feedback campaign in South Austin / Buda / Kyle, seeking founding pilot churches. Dex's demo inventory is the source of truth for shown vs. roadmap.

**Name:** Interconnected, confirmed Aug 24, 2026. Never "Believers In Business," never "Interconnect." Site: www.joininterconnected.com (share the www address; never the bare domain).

## Logo: the gathered dot-cross
Many small dots of varying size and blue shade, gathered into a Latin cross silhouette. Meaning: many individual people, gathered into one body, in the shape of the cross.
- **Sizes.** Full 47-dot mark at 48 px and above. The 13-dot small-size variant only below 48 px (favicon, browser tab, tiny UI). Never on the site, deck, OG card, or app icon.
- **Palettes.** Blues on white · white / light blue on brand blue · light tones on navy. Solid brand blue behind the app icon and link-preview card.
- **Rules.** No letters or text in the mark. No stretching, outlining, or off-palette color. Wordmark is Inter 800. The logo is settled; no redesign proposals unprompted.

## Type and iconography
- Inter, all weights. Headlines 700–800, body 400–500. Sentence case for taglines and buttons.
- Feather-style SVG line icons for all iconography. Groups get a unique line icon each (replaces letter monograms, canon since Aug 2026).
- No emoji. Anywhere. Ever. Not in the app, site, deck, email, or handoffs.
- Aesthetic law: modern but timeless, clean and professional, neutral with one pop color. "Premium, not busy."

## Voice
- **Warm, professional, faith-rooted.** Speak church natively ("the Body"; scripture paraphrased sparingly, with reference). Warmth is added to polish, never traded for it.
- **Honest about stage.** Prototype, founding churches, no pilot data yet. "Half the congregation joins" is an expectation, never a statistic.
- **Never pressuring.** We invite; we don't push. No gamification language, no scarcity beyond "a small group of founding churches."
- **Noah's outreach voice:** short sentences, humble, conversational, direct asks. No em dashes in outreach. Sign-offs: "Much Appreciated," "Grateful for you." Draft strong, expect his rewrite.
- **Proof points, always attributed:** 205M+ American Christians (Pew) · ~384,000 U.S. churches · 70% of U.S. Christians prefer values-aligned businesses (Barna).

## Copy rules (standing law, all lanes)
- **Don't plant negatives the reader hasn't raised.** No "not surveillance" in email. "Shepherding, not surveillance" is for the room and the insights screen.
- **Ask for feedback on the whole idea,** never steer to one part.
- **Outreach asks for wisdom, not meetings, and carries no site link.** "I'm not selling anything" stays in. The demo is shown in person; share the link only after a meeting, to investors, or via the deck QR.
- **Competitors stay unnamed in writing.** Landing page and cold email say "a group text and a shared drive," not GroupMe, Google Drive, or Facebook. Name them only out loud, in reply when the pastor named them first, or in an FAQ answering a real question.
- **Planning Center is always named, as a complement:** "We don't replace Planning Center. It serves your staff; this serves the people in the seats."
- **Roadmap vs. shown.** Launch Sunday kit, weekly digests, encryption, and support SLAs are promises. Say "we provide" or "designed to," never "let me show you."
- **Teammates are name + title** in every handoff (e.g., "Dex, Head of Product Development").
