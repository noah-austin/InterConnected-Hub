**Interconnected**   Brand guidelines · one page	v1 · Aug 24, 2026 · owner: June (Marketing)

*Your church, connected every day of the week.*

| **WHAT WE ARE** **Interconnected **sells a private, invitation-only community platform to individual churches: one siloed network per congregation, where members discover each other's skills, gifts, and needs during the week. Churches subscribe annually; members always join free. **The spine of every message (two-sided): **members join for **community**; the church pays for community **plus insight** (gifts census, member map, integration funnel, care signals, Year in Review). **Stage, said honestly: **interactive prototype, pastor-feedback campaign in South Austin / Buda / Kyle, seeking founding pilot churches. Dex's demo inventory is the source of truth for shown vs. roadmap. **Name: **Interconnected, confirmed Aug 24, 2026. Never “Believers In Business,” never “Interconnect.” Site: **www.joininterconnected.com** (share the www address; never the bare domain). | **LOGO: THE GATHERED DOT-CROSS** Many small dots of varying size and blue shade, gathered into a Latin cross silhouette. Meaning: many individual people, gathered into one body, in the shape of the cross. **Sizes. **Full 47-dot mark at 48 px and above. The 13-dot small-size variant **only below 48 px** (favicon, browser tab, tiny UI). Never on the site, deck, OG card, or app icon. **Palettes. **Blues on white · white / light blue on brand blue or the gradient · light tones on navy. Solid brand blue behind the app icon and OG card. **Rules. **No letters or text in the mark. No stretching, outlining, or off-palette color. Wordmark is Inter 800. The logo is settled; no redesign proposals unprompted. |

**COPY RULES (STANDING LAW, ALL LANES)**

| **Don****'****t plant negatives the reader hasn****'****t raised. **No “not surveillance” in email. “Shepherding, not surveillance” is for the room and the insights screen. **Ask for feedback on the whole idea, **never steer to one part. **Outreach asks for wisdom, not meetings, and carries no site link. **“I'm not selling anything” stays in. The demo is shown in person; share the link only after a meeting, to investors, or via the deck QR. | **Competitors stay unnamed in writing. **Landing page and cold email say “a group text and a shared drive,” not GroupMe, Google Drive, or Facebook. Name them only out loud, in reply when the pastor named them first, or in an FAQ answering a real question. **Planning Center is always named, as a complement: **“We don't replace Planning Center. It serves your staff; this serves the people in the seats.” **Roadmap vs. shown. **Launch Sunday kit, weekly digests, encryption, and support SLAs are promises. Say “we provide” or “designed to,” never “let me show you.” |
