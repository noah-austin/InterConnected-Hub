# Skill — Meeting prep (one page, phone-readable)

> Source: Sonny's brief §6 and handoff rule 13. Worked example: `work/bd/Sonny_Prep_Chris_Smith_FPC.md`. Save each prep as `work/bd/prep-YYYY-MM-DD-<church>.md`.

Noah reads this on his phone in the parking lot. Keep it to one screen-scroll per section.

## Template

```
# Meeting Prep — <Name>, <Church> (<town>)
**When / where:** <date, time, place>  ·  **Status:** <how this meeting came about>

## Snapshot
- Church: <site> · <address> · <phone>
- Size: <~N, services, campuses> → <tier> is the honest quote
- Denomination / flavor; ChMS they run on (Planning Center? Rock?)
- Who you're meeting: <name, role, what they own>; other names to know (exec pastor = likely budget "yes")
- Handle with care: <anything sensitive; don't raise it>

## Personalization hooks
- Their groups philosophy / what groups are called / how members join
- Mission statement, recent sermon series, anything public that connects to our thesis

## The three demo beats
1. Rachel's roofer (feed). "The first thought becomes someone at church instead of Google."
2. Groups as Headquarters. Notes, reading plan, files and sign-ups in one place. Ask how their group leaders run their weeks today.
3. Insights dashboard. Say once, up front: "every number here is sample data for a made-up church." Walk: Year in Review → integration funnel → group gaps → growth geography / member map → gifts census → community health → care signals LAST, said precisely ("every insight is aggregate except this one… activity dates only, never content"). Close with the live admin→phone cross-demo moment.

## Likely objections → responses
<3–5 for this church; pull patterns from pipeline/objections.md>

## Don't overclaim
<pull the relevant lines from work/product/Dex_Demo_Inventory_for_Sonny.md §4>

## The asks, in order
1. Honest read: what's right, wrong, missing; what they'd use and ignore.
2. Pricing (only if it comes up or the meeting is going well): quote the tier, then the three questions verbatim (skills/pricing-three-questions.md).
3. Two referrals, with permission to use their name.
4. If it's strong: founding church? One-page non-binding letter. Don't produce the LOI in the room.

## After
Debrief within the hour (skills/debrief.md). Thank-you drafted for that evening.
```
