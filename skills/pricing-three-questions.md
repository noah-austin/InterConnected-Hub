# Skill — Pricing reaction capture (the three questions)

> Source: Sonny's brief §5, handoff §10, Business Plan v5.1. **Mandatory in every meeting where pricing comes up.** BD captures; it never changes prices. Gus (CFO) owns the tiers.

## Step 1 — Quote the tier honestly
Pick the tier by congregation size. Members always join free.

| Tier | Seats | Annual (indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Small | ≤500 | $4,500 |
| Medium | ≤1,000 | $7,500 |
| Large | ≤2,000 | $12,000 |
| XL | 2,000+ | Custom |

## Step 2 — Ask these three, verbatim
1. **"Does that number feel high, low, or about right for what you saw?"** (gut reaction; capture verbatim)
2. **"What does your church currently spend annually on tools like this — giving platform, church app, ChMS?"** (budget anchor)
3. **"Who besides you would need to say yes to a number like that, and what would they ask?"** (buying-process map)

## Step 3 — Log it
One row per church in `pipeline/pricing-reactions.md`: date, church, persona, tier quoted, the three answers verbatim, notes.

Rules: don't negotiate, don't discount, don't defend the number in the room. The point is clean data. The tiers are a hypothesis until this table has real rows.
