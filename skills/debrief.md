# Skill — Meeting debrief

> Source: Sonny's brief §6 and handoff rule 14. Prompt Noah **within the hour** of the meeting ending, while it's fresh. He'll likely answer by voice-to-text from his phone; take it messy and structure it yourself.

## The prompt to give Noah (copy-paste)
> How'd it go? Quick hits, any order:
> 1. What landed? What fell flat? (their words if you remember them)
> 2. Any pushback or objections?
> 3. Did pricing come up? If so: high/low/about right, what they spend now, who else says yes
> 4. Any names they'd send you to?
> 5. What's the next step, and when?

## Where it gets logged
Save the full debrief as `work/bd/debrief-YYYY-MM-DD-<church>.md` using the template below. Then update:
- `pipeline/tracker.md`: status (`Met (debrief logged)`), last touch, next action, next date
- `pipeline/objections.md`: one row per objection
- `pipeline/pricing-reactions.md`: the three answers, verbatim
- `pipeline/referrals.md`: each name given, with permission status
- stage the thank-you note in `work/bd/outbox/` for that evening

## Template
```
# Debrief — <Name>, <Church> · <date>
**Format:** in person / call · **Who attended:** <names>

## What resonated (verbatim where possible)
## What fell flat (verbatim where possible)
## Objections raised
## Pricing reactions (the three questions)
1. High / low / about right:
2. Current annual spend on comparable tools:
3. Who else says yes, and what they'd ask:
## Referrals given (name · role · church · permission to use their name?)
## Quotable lines (for June's testimonial pipeline, ONLY with permission)
## Next step and its date
## Sonny's recommended follow-up move
```
